Critter Overlay 3.0 is a new app, rebuilt from the ground up. The critters
are drawn and animated afresh, and there's a game to it now: they come to you
as you focus.

## Install

Download **CritterOverlaySetup-3.0.0.exe** below and open it. The installer
isn't signed yet: if Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**From v2.0:** install straight over it. If v2.0 is running, the installer
closes it. Your settings and the critters you'd seen come with you. Custom
critters from v2.0 stay safe on your PC but don't show in 3.0 yet.

## What's new

- **Ten critters**, all new: kitten, rabbit, duckling, hedgehog, turtle,
  squirrel, otter and panda, plus two Legendary visitors (and their secret
  colours) to discover.
- **Focus brings company.** Critters arrive as you work, nap when you step
  away and wake when you're back.
- **Rarity:** Common, Rare and Epic looks for every critter, and Rare Hour
  once a day at a time you choose.
- **Berries and the Shop:** earn berries for focus time and spend them on
  clothes, dyes, treats and showpieces (beds and a cottage where critters nap).
- **The Collection:** everything you've met, with the visitors kept secret
  until you meet them.
- **Pairs:** critters greet, groom and play with each other.
- **New Settings, tray panel and welcome**, in light and dark.
- **One-click updates** from now on.
- **Quieter on your PC:** fewer frames when nothing's moving, and much less
  work while paused or hidden.
- **Help when something's wrong:** Settings › System › Help › Copy diagnostics.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your computer. It checks for updates on GitHub once a
day (you can turn that off); nothing about you or your critters is sent.

## Known issues

- The installer and app aren't signed, so Windows SmartScreen warns on first
  install.
- Critters live on the main screen only; walking across every screen is
  planned for 3.1.
- Custom critters from v2.0 don't appear yet (they're kept, and planned for a
  later update). Uninstalling with "delete my data" removes them as well.
- Touch screens and pens may not count as you being there, so critters may
  nap while you use one.
- Clicking just above a curled-up hedgehog, a rolling otter or a rolling panda
  can still pop it.
- The first time a new look arrives it can stutter for a moment.
- Notes that would show while paused or in a full-screen app are skipped, not
  saved for later.
- Settings and the tray don't follow a light/dark switch in Windows until
  they're reopened.
- The tray panel can't be used with the keyboard yet.
- On a 1280-pixel-wide screen, the Collection's sightings diary runs past
  its column.
