Critter Overlay 3.2 adds codes, and the first thing they unlock: the Supporter Pack.

## What's new

- **Codes.** Settings › System › Codes has a box to paste a code into, and the
  Shop has a **Have a code?** link. Codes are checked on your computer: no
  account, no sign-in, and nothing is sent anywhere.
- **The Supporter Pack: the Starlight set.** A Moon nightcap, a Starry capelet
  and Star glasses for every critter, the Moon cradle (a showpiece critters
  curl up and nap in) and Royal blue, a dye for any dyeable clothes. It's looks
  only: nothing in it touches berries, luck or rarity. It's the way to support
  the app, and it's on [itch.io](https://critteroverlay.itch.io/supporter-pack).
  Critter Overlay itself stays free.
- **Creator codes**, for people who feature Critter Overlay in their videos.
  They come with the Supporter Pack, a Director's beret, 25,000 berries for
  dressing critters on camera, and creator mode: **Call a critter** from the
  tray brings any everyday critter in its Common, Rare or Epic look, and it
  doesn't count in your Collection. Creator mode has an off switch in
  Settings › System › Creator.

## Install

Update from inside the app, or download **CritterOverlaySetup-3.2.0.exe**
below and open it. The installer isn't signed yet: if Windows says "Windows
protected your PC", choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Install straight over 3.1, 3.0 or v2.0. Your settings, Collection and berries
are kept.
